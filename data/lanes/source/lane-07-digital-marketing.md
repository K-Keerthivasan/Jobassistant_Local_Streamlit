# Lane 7 | Digital marketing

NOC 11202, TEER 1. Track 2, and the strongest CEC-feeding code in the whole search. Read `00-index.md` for the Status conventions and tier rules.

**PR note.** 11202 is TEER 1, which outranks most of Track 1 on paper. **No RCIP community list covers it, anywhere, so never tag a lane 7 role RCIP.** AIP does cover it with no occupation list, which makes an Atlantic digital marketing role at a designated employer one of the strongest combinations available. Ontario's Workforce Priority stream also takes it with a qualifying offer.

## The NOC trap, read before grading

A "Marketing Coordinator" that is really scheduling, data entry and inbox management maps to a TEER 4 clerical code, not 11202. Read the body for **campaign ownership, budget or spend responsibility, analytics, and strategy input**. If none are present, skip it. A marketing title carrying a hard sales quota is 64100 and not PR-eligible. Skip.

## Not required

Brand strategy ownership and team management. Both are senior and fail the seniority gate anyway.

## Honest positioning

He runs a digital marketing agency. That is the headline and it should lead, because very few junior applicants have owned client outcomes. **Lead with paid media, campaign management and analytics. Do not lead with camera work**, which is the standing instruction for this lane and it matters: a video-first framing reads as a production hire, not a marketing hire, and costs him the 11202 mapping.

The honest gap is scale and certification. Agency client budgets are not enterprise budgets, so state the real numbers and let them be what they are rather than implying more. Most in-scope postings grade Tier A or Tier B. **Google Ads certification is the highest-value small move in this lane**, and GA4 specifically is worth confirming since it is named in nearly every posting.

## Interview watch

Expect "walk me through a campaign you ran and what the numbers did". Have one with real figures, including one that underperformed and what you changed. Expect a question on how you would spend a fixed budget across channels. Owning an agency invites the same question as lane 6 about whether he will leave to run it, so have the answer ready.
