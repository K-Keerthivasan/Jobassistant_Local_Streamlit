# Lane 6 | Technical sales, solutions and implementation

NOC 62100, TEER 2. Secondary lane, opportunistic. Read `00-index.md` for the Status conventions and tier rules.

**PR note.** 62100 is on the Altona and Rhineland RCIP list, and AIP covers it from a designated Atlantic employer. Ontario's Workforce Priority stream takes it with a qualifying offer. **The trap in this lane is the NOC, not the skill:** non-technical sales is 64100 or 64101 and is not PR-eligible. Only genuinely technical seats map to 62100.

## The gate before the matrix

A role enters this lane only if the body shows real technical content: demos he would run, systems he would configure, integrations he would scope, or customers he would implement for. **A posting carrying a hard sales quota is a sales seat.** It maps to 64100, it is not PR-eligible, and it is dropped rather than graded. Read the compensation paragraph: on-target earnings and commission structure without technical duties is the tell.

## Not required

Quota-carrying sales experience. If a posting requires it, that posting is 64100 and out of scope.

## Honest positioning

Running K2 Digital Media is the argument for this lane, and it is a genuinely good one: he sells, scopes, delivers and supports technical work for real clients under his own name. That is the entire job of a solutions or implementation seat at junior level. The gap is that he has done it as an owner rather than inside a sales organisation, so he has no experience of a pipeline, a quota, or a formal sales process.

Most in-scope postings here grade Tier A or Tier B. Keep this lane opportunistic: it is not a priority lane, it should never displace a priority-lane role, and the NOC trap means a careless row here does real damage by suggesting a PR pathway that is not there.

## Interview watch

Expect "sell me this product" and expect a question about a time a customer was unhappy. The Bill Gosling year and the K2 client work both answer the second one well. Be ready for a direct question about whether he actually wants sales, since owning an agency reads as someone who may leave to run it full time. Have an honest answer.
