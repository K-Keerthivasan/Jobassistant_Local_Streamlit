# Lane 8 | Social media and content

NOC 11202, TEER 1. Track 2. Read `00-index.md` for the Status conventions and tier rules.

**PR note.** Same code as lane 7, so the same position: TEER 1, strong for CEC, **never RCIP-eligible**, covered by AIP from a designated Atlantic employer, and covered by Ontario's Workforce Priority stream with a qualifying offer.

## The NOC trap, read before grading

Same trap as lane 7 and it fires harder here, because social coordinator postings skew junior and administrative. Look for **content ownership, channel strategy, performance responsibility and budget or spend input**. A posting that is scheduling someone else's content and answering comments is clerical, maps to TEER 4, and is skipped.

## Not required

Team management, agency account direction. Senior, fails the seniority gate.

## Honest positioning

His strongest asset here is that he has built and run channels rather than only posted to them. K2_Press is a publishing system he wrote; ClubJunk and JKR are channels he grew himself. That combination of operator and builder is rare at this level and it is the thing to lead with.

The gap worth naming is corporate context: he has run his own brands and small business clients, not a company channel inside a marketing department with approvals, brand guidelines and legal review. Most in-scope postings grade Tier A. Be careful about the NOC trap here more than anywhere else in Track 2, since a clerical social posting looks identical to a real one until you read the body.

## Interview watch

Expect to be asked for your accounts. Have the links ready and be honest about follower numbers rather than letting them be discovered. Expect "what would you post for us in your first month", which rewards having actually looked at their channels beforehand.
